# Agents demonstration

## Frame 1
status: built
src: index.html
Duration: 28 seconds; one continuous product viewport.
Rules: fixed product viewport, cursor cues and native app interaction states.
English conversations carry the explanation without an additional caption band.

- 0–2: establish Jarvis, Scout and Archivist in their real island.
- 2–7: open Jarvis's real agent card; assign the launch check.
- 7–11: Scout asks Archivist through the native conversation renderer.
- 11–15: Archivist replies, with figures oriented toward each other.
- 15–21: open Scout's real chat; refine the task directly.
- 21–26: Jarvis returns the combined result.
- 26–28: return to the opening island and cursor position for the loop.

Nine real controls are recorded: Jarvis at 2s, its composer at 2.3s, Send at
3.6s, Close at 7s, Scout at 15s, its composer at 15.3s, Send at 17s, Jarvis in
the card rail at 21s, and Close at 26s. Each view responds 140ms after its
visible press. The real composer contains the complete text before Send.
These controls supply the cursor positions; there are no authored pixel guesses.

Production UI and art come directly from the app source. Capture adapters
bind their clock and stores to film time; demo content is synthetic. The
website presents the render as a native autoplay video with no player chrome.
